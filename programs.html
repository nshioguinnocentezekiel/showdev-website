Our Programs

Intervention programs designed to reduce poverty and empower women, youth and communities.

Health

Promoting access to quality, inclusive, and affordable healthcare services. We focus on primary healthcare, sexual and reproductive health, HIV/AIDS, TB, and Malaria (ATM), maternal and child health, and community health education.

Education

Advancing equitable access to quality education and skills development. Our interventions target out-of-school children, girl-child education, literacy, vocational training, and school WASH programs.

Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH)

Ensuring sustainable access to safe water, improved sanitation, and hygiene practices. We work with government and communities to reduce water-borne diseases and promote climate-resilient WASH infrastructure.

Climate Change Adaptation and Food Security

Building community resilience to climate change while improving food systems. We support climate-smart agriculture, sustainable livelihoods, environmental protection, and nutrition security.

Protection

Safeguarding the rights and dignity of vulnerable populations. We address gender-based violence (GBV), child protection, safeguarding, and psychosocial support to ensure safe and inclusive communities. SHOWDEV has zero-tolerance for Sexual Exploitation and Abuse, and Sexual Harassment (SEA & SH).

Livelihood and Economic Empowerment

Promoting sustainable livelihoods and economic opportunities for women and youths. We provide skills training, entrepreneurship support, financial inclusion, and market linkages to reduce poverty and increase self-reliance.
