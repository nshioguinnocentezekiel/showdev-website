Our Programs

Intervention programs designed to reduce poverty and empower women, youth and communities.

Health

Grassroots awareness campaigns on HIV/AIDS, TB, Malaria and other related health areas. Care and support for people living with HIV/AIDS (PLWHA), and campaigns against discrimination and stigmatization.

Peace Resolution

Conceptualized methods and processes to resolve conflict and facilitate peaceful outcomes in communities.

Gender Equity

Mainstreaming gender perspective in all policies and programs through systematic gender analysis, enforcement of CEDAW, and enhancement of national and state policy on women.

Women & Girls Empowerment

Eliminating discrimination against women, educating on gender issues, promoting political and economic empowerment, and motivating women and youth into income-generating activities through skills acquisition and agricultural training.

Youth Development

Building socially responsible young people as role models — promoting positive behavior change, psychosocial support, and positive attitudes towards people living with HIV/AIDS.

Community Participation & Infrastructure

Reducing poverty in rural communities by strengthening participation, economic empowerment, and access to health education, safe water, and sanitation.

Advocacy

Advocating for policy implementation and affirmative action on the rights of women, youth and children, free from harmful traditional practices and customs.

Capacity Building

Institutional strengthening, community needs assessments, project and conflict management, and monitoring and evaluation to sustain long-term impact.
