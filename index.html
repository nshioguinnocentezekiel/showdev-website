Who We Are

Sustainable Hope Foundation for Women and Youth Development (SHOWDEV) is a nonprofit, non-governmental organization born out of a strong desire to assist in the personal development of women, youth and people in the communities — with a strategy aimed at skills acquisition for meaningful contribution to communal empowerment and development, culminating in poverty reduction.

Our Mission

To empower women, girls, youths and vulnerable populations through agriculture, livelihood skills acquisition and training, promote gender equity and justice, and advocate for accessible, quality healthcare and education.

Our Vision

A world where women, girls, youth and individuals have the skills and resources to build a better future for themselves, their families and their communities — where gender equity and justice is a reality, and access to quality education and healthcare is universal.

Our Focus Areas

Our intervention programs are rooted in the following pillars.

Health

HIV/AIDS, TB and Malaria awareness, care and support, and campaigns against stigmatization.

Gender Equity

Mainstreaming gender perspective across policy and enforcement of women's rights.

Women & Girls Empowerment

Skills acquisition, economic opportunity, and leadership development for women.

Youth Development

Positive behavior change, psychosocial support, and role-model building for young people.

Peace Building

Conflict resolution processes that facilitate peaceful outcomes in communities.

Advocacy

Policy implementation and affirmative action on the rights of women, youth and children.
