About SHOWDEV

A center for empowerment and community development.

Our Story

Sustainable Hope Foundation for Women and Youths Development (SHOWDEV) is a nonprofit, non-governmental organization that deals with issues concerning women, youth and people in the communities. SHOWDEV provides varied services geared towards improving the quality of life of its beneficiaries for sustainable development.

SHOWDEV was born out of a strong desire to assist in the personal development of women, youth and people in the communities, with a strategy aimed at skills acquisition for meaningful contribution to communal empowerment and development — culminating in poverty reduction.

Mission

To empower women, girls, youths, and vulnerable populations through sustainable agriculture, livelihood and economic empowerment, and skills development. We advance gender equity and justice, and promote access to quality health and education services for improved well-being and self-reliance.

Vision

A world where women, girls, youths, and vulnerable individuals have the skills, resources, and opportunities to build better futures for themselves and their communities; where gender equity and justice are realized, and access to quality education and healthcare is universal.

Our Implementation Strategies

  • Community Sensitization and Dialogue — We promote awareness and positive social behavioral change.
  • Training and Capacity Development — We build skills of staff, partners, and community structures.
  • Community Mobilization — We ensure participation, ownership and sustainability.
  • Advocacy — We influence policies and increase government commitment.
  • Monitoring, Evaluation, Learning and Information Management — For data-driven decision making.
  • Research and Documentation — We generate evidence and document best practices.
  • Peace Building and Conflict Resolution — We promote social cohesion and peaceful coexistence.
  • Partnership and Collaboration — We leverage resources and ensure sustainability.

Governance & Management

SHOWDEV is governed by the Management Team, supported by the Executive Board. The Management Executive is headed by the Executive Director, the apex decision-making personnel, supported by the Advisory Board, Programme Manager, Finance Manager, Administration/Procurement Manager, and Associate Manager. The Executive Director reports to the Chairman of the Board annually.
